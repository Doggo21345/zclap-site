Two separate costs

The Informatica subscription. Informatica prices its cloud services, including MDM SaaS, in Informatica Processing Units (IPUs), a consumption-based model. You buy that from Informatica or its reseller, and it depends on your volumes and services.

The implementation. The design, build, data loading, testing and integration work, done by your team, a partner like us, or both. This page covers what moves that number.

What drives the implementation cost

These are the inputs our questionnaire asks about, because they decide most of the price.

Project type

A new build, an on-prem to SaaS modernization, a domain expansion on a live hub, or a replacement of another MDM. Expansions are usually the cheapest because the platform is already there.

Biggest single factor

Entities and domains

Customer, supplier, product, provider, location. Each domain brings its own model, rules and stewards.

Scales with count

Sources

How many systems feed the hub, and how clean and consistent they are.

Scales with count and quality

Consuming systems

Where golden records need to go: Salesforce, SAP, Workday, Oracle, a warehouse. Real-time integrations cost more than batch.

Scales with count and latency

Hierarchies and matching

Multi-level customer or product hierarchies and complex match rules take design and testing time.

Scales with complexity

Custom code

For modernizations: user exits, Data Director customizations and workflows that have to be redesigned on SaaS.

Often the hidden driver

Why we can fix the price

Because we scope around one or two bounded use cases, most projects come down to the short list of inputs above. Once we know them, we can commit to a fixed price and a timeline before any open-ended discovery.

Some projects do need discovery first, or carry a technical risk specific to your environment. When that happens we say so and shape the work to fit, rather than force a number that won't hold.

Common questions

How much does an Informatica MDM implementation cost?

It depends on the project type, the number of domains, sources and consuming systems, hierarchy and match complexity, and any custom code. Answer the 5-minute questionnaire and we'll send a cost range for your project within 72 hours.

Is the Informatica license included in your estimate?

No. Our estimate covers the implementation. The Informatica subscription is priced by Informatica in IPUs and bought separately.

Why are MDM projects known for going over budget?

Open-ended scope. Programs that try to master every domain at once, without a business outcome to stop at, keep growing. Bounded use cases keep the inputs known and the price fixed.

What's the cheapest way to start?

One domain and one outcome, or an expansion of a hub you already run. Both reuse what's in place.
