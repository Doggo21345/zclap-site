FAQ

Questions buyers ask us first.

How can you commit to a fixed price for MDM?

We scope around one or more bounded use cases, so most projects come down to a short set of known inputs: entities, sources, consuming systems, hierarchies and volumes. When a project needs discovery or carries one-off technical risk, we say so plainly and shape the work to fit.

Isn't MDM slow and expensive?

That reputation comes from on-prem builds that took years. Informatica MDM SaaS removes the servers, the scaling and most of the build time. The fastest way past the doubt is a real number, which is what the questionnaire gives you.

We already run Informatica MDM on-prem. Can you modernize it without breaking what works?

Yes. We plan for your legacy golden records and prove parity before cutover, protecting what your current hub delivers instead of just copying records across.

We're on a non-Informatica or homegrown MDM. Can you replace it?

Yes. Competitive replacements have more project-specific variables, so we scope them on a short call. A national health insurer we worked with exited its incumbent ahead of a hard renewal deadline.

Which systems do you integrate MDM with?

Commonly Salesforce, SAP, Workday and Oracle, in batch or real time. Every integration is different, so we scope yours before quoting.

What happens after I submit the questionnaire?

Our team reviews it, not an automated calculator. Within 72 hours you get a PDF with a cost range, a timeline in weeks and a complexity rating. There's no obligation, and final pricing comes in a formal proposal after a 45-minute call.
