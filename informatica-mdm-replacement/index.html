Questions that decide it

What has to be rebuilt? Switching platforms means rebuilding the data model, match and merge rules, stewardship workflows and every integration. Moving within Informatica, from on-prem to MDM SaaS, keeps most of the design and the team's skills.

What does the current platform do well? Years of tuned matching encode business decisions. Whatever you choose, carry those decisions across and test them on real duplicates.

What's the deadline? Renewal dates drive many switches. A national health insurer we worked with moved two domains off a non-Informatica cloud MDM and left its incumbent ahead of a hard renewal deadline.

What will the business use it for? Platforms differ less than projects do. The outcome you scope for matters more than the logo on the hub.

Common questions

Is Informatica MDM better than Reltio?

It depends on your data, your other platforms and your team. Informatica is strongest where you also need its integration, data quality and governance services, or deep matching and hierarchy control. We're Informatica specialists, so weigh our view accordingly, and price both paths.

Can you migrate our golden records from another MDM?

Yes. We seed the new hub from your current golden records and prove parity before cutover.

How do you price a replacement?

Replacements have more project-specific variables than other work, so we scope them on a short call, then give you a number you can plan against.

What changed for Informatica MDM after Salesforce bought Informatica?

Salesforce completed the acquisition on 18 November 2025 and has said it will connect Informatica's MDM, integration and governance with its own data platform. For roadmap specifics, ask your Informatica account team.
